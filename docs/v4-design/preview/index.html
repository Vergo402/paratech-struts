Logo & Mark — FS monogram (new identity · Open Q#10)

New identity, not a refresh (overrides essay 02's "refresh the P"). The F is built from rescue struts (tube body + base-plate feet + locking collar); the S from cut 4×4 lumber fastened with gusset plates, the way a T-shore is fastened. One ink — steel vs. wood reads from form (round/smooth vs. square/gusseted), never color. The mark appears only as gold-on-slate or dark-goldenrod-on-white.
