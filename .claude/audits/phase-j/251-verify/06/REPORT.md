## Harness lessons (for later passes)
- Playwright glob `'**:9099/**'` does NOT match the Auth emulator URL. Use a regex route, `ctx.route(/:9099\//, ...)`. My first delay/abort attempts silently did nothing. Shots 07 and 08 were taken after the response returned and show an idle button. The valid busy-state shots are 35 and 39. Offline shots 30 and 31 were taken before I fixed this and are superseded by 30b and 31b.
- HARNESS `goOffline` does not survive a cold reload. For the cold-reload offline case I used: Auth requests aborted by regex route, the `:9000` WebSocket closed with `page.routeWebSocket`, and `navigator.onLine` forced false by an init script. Control: a guest profile under the same conditions shows the "Sign in" nav (shot 30c).
- Magic link: take the link from `GET http://localhost:9099/emulator/v1/projects/fieldshore-database/oobCodes`. Fetch it with `redirect:'manual'` and `page.goto` the 303 `Location` (it points back at `localhost:5200/auth?...oobCode=...`).
- A dialog-observer positive control: `page.evaluate(()=>alert('ctl'))` was captured by the `dialog` listener ("alert: ctl"), so the "no alert()" checks below could have failed.
- The page reloads on sign-in and sign-out. The "Signing you in…" text is only catchable with an init-script poller that logs into `sessionStorage`.
- A signed-in profile shows a first-run coach dialog ("Measure the opening", Skip/Next) on the tab shell after account creation. A fresh guest does not get it (shot 38).
