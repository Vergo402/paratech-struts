# BRIEF 15 — Secured / Returned

**Spec:** `docs/v4-design/09-workflows/15-secured-returned.md` · **Phase G issue:** #224 (epic #135) · **Preconditions:** workflow 14 (Runner).

**Summary.** The last two stops of a shore point's life. A point in the Runner lane is slid forward to **Wood Shore Secured** (strut confirmed set). Hours later, when the gear is physically collected, an officer taps **Remove & Return Equipment** on the Secured card, confirms a modal that warns "This cannot be undone", and the point becomes **Strut Equipment Returned**: a read-only terminal card, with every deployed piece (strut, plates, extensions) back in its source rig's available count. Secured can still be slid back to Runner; Returned cannot be undone. Both transitions are per-card (not grouped).

## Setup (build via the app's own UI, phone viewport 390x844)

1. Sign up at `/auth` (Create Account), then Create department (`/create-department`). Use a fresh department.
2. `/inventory` -> "Add apparatus" (e.g. "Rescue 2") -> "Add equipment" on that rig (sheet title "Add equipment — Rescue 2"): stock at least 2 of a strut (e.g. LS 203 / a model that fits your measurement) and the plates/extensions the deploy will pull. Record the Rescue 2 strut row's **available** and **held** numbers (the `N of M available` row, `EquipmentRow`).
3. `/operations` -> "Start Operation" (needs manageOperations; creator is Admin). Add two shore points ("+ Add Shore Point"): SP-A a T-Shore, SP-B a second point (to prove the other card is unaffected; optionally a 3-Post to check group non-lockstep). Use "Assign equipment" on each Pending card to deploy from Rescue 2.
4. Walk each to Runner: Equipment Assigned -> Strut Set -> Cutting Station (`/operations` Cutting Station card controls: "Slide to mark Cut Done", "Slide to send to Runner") -> Runner lane. Slider labels: "Slide to set Strut Set", "Slide to send to Cutting Station", "Slide to set Wood Shore Secured".
5. Second device (cross-device claims): origin B `http://127.0.0.1:5200`, Join department by code, open `/operations` and `/inventory`. Device A = `localhost:5200`.
6. Note: the board lane headers read **Secured** and **Returned** (short labels, `cardParts.ts`); the lane `<section>` aria-label and the card text use the full names **Wood Shore Secured** / **Strut Equipment Returned**. On phone the lanes sit under a "Wood & return" divider.
7. **Slide vs button:** on a desktop browser with a mouse (`(hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`) the Slider renders a plain button instead of a slide (ADR-035): "Set Wood Shore Secured" / "Back to Runner". On touch emulation it is a slide. Drive whichever the viewport yields and record which.

## Superseded / Deferred

- **Slide is the only commit gesture; the SR Advance/Step-back button pair is gone.** Superseded by ADR-026 (#248 KB-5): the spec's accessibility text assumes assistive-tech operability of the slide; now AT can observe but not drive status slides. The Remove & Return button and modal stay fully operable (scope guard). Replacement behavior covered by 15-01, 15-03.
- **Mouse devices get a button instead of a slide.** Superseded by ADR-035 for the Runner -> Secured advance and the step-back (labels "Set Wood Shore Secured", "Back to Runner"). Covered inside 15-01/15-03.
- **Inventory "increments in the commit transaction", "available count", "transaction guard §NEW-7" (Open Q 3).** Superseded by ADR-041 (#499): `available` is no longer stored; it is `quantity - held`, derived from the folded event log, signed, and the return simply ends the point's hold (`status !== 'returned'`). A duplicate return is a deterministic reducer no-op, not a counter guard. Covered by 15-13, 15-20. Negative availability ("over-allocated") is possible if another problem exists but not from this workflow.
- **Whole-assembly return.** Superseded by ADR-033: Remove & Return restores the strut AND every tracked plate/extension, each to its own source rig; the modal copy says so. Covered by 15-08, 15-13.
- **Returned card keeps its BOM as history** (ADR-033/#224 build): the spec's "deployed-strut identity through both states". Covered by 15-14. Re-sourcing a returned point is blocked ("cannot re-source returned equipment") - not drivable from the UI, no criterion.
- **Unreturned-equipment warning at End Operation** (gate review M3 shipped v4.0, extended by ADR-041 D5 "All equipment is back on the rigs" checkbox). Cross-effect with Returned in 15-16; the rest belongs to workflow 16.
- **Broadcast counts.** ADR-040 addendum: no app-side Broadcast build for v4.0 - TV is the normal Operations board screen-mirrored. The spec's "Broadcast" row is satisfied by 15-17/15-19 on the normal screens.
- **Returned-lane collapse / archival treatment.** Deferred to workflow #238 / Phase H (spec Open Q 1; 20-operations.md Open Q 3). Build default: the lane starts expanded. Do not fail for either.
- **Role gate wording and enforcement.** Not superseded; no later decision found. Kept Testable (15-23): a missing gate is NOT-BUILT. (ADR-017 says the fireground stays position-gated; the gate-review items 194/200 leave the mechanism as an open question.)
- **External-equipment copy variant** is "Phase H owns the copy" (Open Q 2); no later ADR found; kept Testable (15-24), likely N/A or NOT-BUILT.
- **Per-line Remove / removing a stocked rig** (#513 ruling, #525 v4.1) - adjacent to returned equipment (a rig with anything held cannot be removed) but out of scope here.

## Risks for the QA agent

- **Copy differs from the spec and that is not a bug:** modal body wording (15-08), announcements (15-04, 15-12), lane header text "Secured"/"Returned" vs the full names in region labels. Record exact strings; only the title "Return equipment to inventory?", the button names, and "This cannot be undone." are fixed.
- **The Secured advance is a slide on touch but a button with a mouse** (ADR-035). A slide drive with synthetic mouse events will not work; use the button or pointer events / touch emulation.
- **Phone lane grouping:** lanes are inside a "Wood & return" divider; a collapsed lane hides its cards (chevron toggles `aria-expanded`). Empty lanes are "quiet" (count badge 0, no message).
- **Inventory numbers are derived and signed.** `available` rises only when the point's status becomes Returned; step-back to Runner and Secured itself never change it. A deployed point's strut counts as held until Returned. Total `quantity` never moves.
- **Cross-rig BOM:** a shore can pull plates from a different rig than its strut; check every consumed row, not only the strut.
- **Two origins = two devices.** Sync is cloud receipt-ordered (ADR-041): the device that reached the cloud first wins; a loser sees a quiet "Synced - N of your changes had no effect" line, never a modal. That is expected in 15-20 and 15-25.
- **Router quirks:** `<Link>` taps may not route under synthetic clicks (use `location.assign`); lane/card headers are plain buttons.
- **Role gate (15-23):** with the creator signed in as Admin, back-office permission is not the same as an ICS position. Check My Role (Command tab) before concluding.
