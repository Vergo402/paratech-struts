# #251 criteria brief 14 — Runner

- **Spec:** `docs/v4-design/09-workflows/14-runner.md` (Phase G issue #223).
- **Drafted:** 2026-10-13 against HEAD 903a68e on `v4-redesign`.
- **Summary.** A cut strut has been sent to the Runner lane. The runner carries it to the opening and slides the card to Wood Shore Secured: one slide, one point at a time (even for a 3-Post group; after Send to Runner every move is individual), no confirm. A step-back slide on the same card returns it to the Cutting Station queue, keeping its "Cut done" flag and its place in line. Since the spec was written: the station gained named saws (#354) and #475 made a runner-to-cutting step-back drop the saw claim (the cut rejoins the shared queue unclaimed); on a mouse the slides become tap-once buttons (ADR-035); the "assistive tech gets a button twin" promise was reversed (ADR-026); and the Runner role gate in the spec is not an app rule (ADR-017: fireground gating is by ICS position, and the Operations board contains no such check).

## Setup (build first, via the app's own UI)

Routes: one Operations route (`/operations`, tab "Operations", `src/app/routes/operations.tsx`). The Runner lane is a lane on the **Board** segment of the "Operations view" control (`Segmented`, aria-label "Operations view", options Board / Cutting). On phone the seven lanes sit under three dividers: "Strut placement", "Cutting", "Wood & return" (Runner, Wood Shore Secured, Strut Equipment Returned). There is no separate Runner screen. Lane header names: "Runner", "Wood Shore Secured" (`STATUS_LABELS`).

1. Harness per `HARNESS.md` (emulators + `dev:emu` :5200). `/auth` → Create Account (`qa251-14-1@example.test`) → Create department ("QA Dept 14"). Creator = Admin.
2. Inventory: apparatus "Rescue 2" stocked with at least 4x LS 304 (or LS 203 if the catalog has it; any model is fine, record what you use) plus 2 more struts. See the `05` brief and the #475 recipe in `.claude/audits/phase-j/step1-fixes/475/REPORT.md` ("Setup").
3. Start an operation ("QA Op 14"). Turn "Utilize Pending Card" off for one-step inline deploy, or deploy from the Pending card.
4. Add shore points ("+ Add Shore Point"):
   - SP1: T-Shore, Div 1 / Side A, opening 5 ft 0 in (expected cut 51½″). Ungrouped.
   - SP2: T-Shore, opening 3 ft 6 in (cut 33½″). Ungrouped.
   - SP3–SP5: a 3-Post group of three, opening 5 ft 0 in (cut 47½″). For the "individual, not group-wide" criterion.
5. Assign Rescue 2 struts to every point, then walk SP1, SP2 and SP3–SP5 to Strut Set, then to Cutting Station (Board slide "Slide to send to Cutting Station"; the 3-Post moves together). In the Cutting view: Mark Cut Done, then Send to Runner on SP1, SP2 and SP3 (only SP3; SP4 and SP5 stay in Cutting). Start the Runner steps with SP1, SP2, SP3 in the Runner lane.
6. Second device (cross-device criteria): Device A `http://localhost:5200`, Device B `http://127.0.0.1:5200`, second member of the same department (join by invite code, see `08` brief). Both on `/operations`, Board view.
7. Surface: phone 390x844 unless noted. On a mouse-driven pane the slides render as tap-once buttons (ADR-035: "Set Wood Shore Secured" / "Back to Cutting Station"); note which form you drove. To drive the real slide, emulate touch (390 wide mobile preset).

## Risks for the QA agent

- **Tap-once buttons on a mouse pane.** Slides become buttons (ADR-035); the "Set Wood Shore Secured" / "Back to Cutting Station" labels are not bugs. Use the mobile preset to see real slides. The step-back button on mouse reads "Back to Cutting Station"; with a touch slide it is "Slide back to Cutting Station".
- **Group stays individual after Send to Runner.** Only the pre-runner zone (Equipment Assigned ↔ Strut Set ↔ Cutting) moves a whole group. Seeing SP4/SP5 left in Cutting after SP3 advances is correct.
- **"Cut done" returns on the bounce.** This is intentional (#223, Phase J ruling 2026-10 for #475) even though it can look stale. The cutter can clear it with "Slide back — clear Cut Done". Known deferred glitch in the ledger: a bounced cut-done cut shows no done marker under "Up next" rows (only on the hero/board card), and dev StrictMode may double-claim on first mount.
- **Saw tag disappears on bounce.** Correct per #475; do not report as lost state.
- **Strut model hidden in Cutting.** The model suffix is suppressed while a card is in Cutting but returns in Runner (14-04).
- **Announcement wording** is the build's ("Shore point — now Wood Shore Secured."); the spec's longer VoiceOver strings are aspirational. Report as SPEC-DRIFT, not FAIL, unless the live region is missing altogether.
- **Role gate probably absent.** No runner position check exists in `OperationsBoard.tsx`; a non-IC member can likely slide. Record NOT-BUILT or SPEC-DRIFT (ADR-017) with the evidence of what the second member could do; do not file as a security bug without the orchestrator's ruling.
- **Lane collapsed.** Lanes can be collapsed; an advance expands the destination lane (`expandLane`). If a card seems to vanish, expand the lane before judging.
- **Scroll-into-view.** After a commit the board scrolls to the moved card; a screenshot taken mid-scroll may show a stale frame (use the three probes in HARNESS).
