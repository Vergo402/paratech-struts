## Why this plan changed shape

The earlier draft of `v4.0.0-plan.md` was built around the Surfside TTX-2 hotwash: 4 OPs, 36 sim hours, 494 peak personnel, 66 shore points, 63 findings. That sim was designed-as-upper-bound — a stretch test to find where the app breaks. It ended up shaping v4.0 scope toward federal Type I/II response. That was scope creep.

The reframe pulls v4.0 back to the case that actually represents 95%+ of expected use: a small or mid municipal fire department running a Type IV-V structural collapse incident. One department, one IC, 2-4 apparatus, 5-20 shore points, hours-to-shift duration. Examples: car into building, residential partial collapse, light commercial partial collapse.

The Surfside findings are not discarded — they're sorted. Scale-agnostic correctness bugs stay in v4.0. Federal-scale features and multi-agency infrastructure move to a clearly-labeled "Federal Future" bucket at the bottom of this plan.

The release train collapses from **6 releases / 13-15 weeks** to **3 releases / 5-6 weeks**.

These are real bugs at any scale — a 6-SP house collapse exhibits them the same way a 66-SP TF op did.

| **IP-005 / IP-022 (partial)** | NIMS terminology cleanup | `group` → `assignedResource` and `'Strut Placed'` → `'Strut Set'` are doctrine corrections at any scale. |

### Findings that move to Federal Future (deferred)

These exist because Surfside-scale federal incidents exist. They are real findings, but a municipal department running residential partial collapses doesn't encounter them. See "Federal Future" section at the end of this plan for the full catalog with reactivation triggers.

Summary: multi-agency identity & per-agency write scope (IP-001/002/003/004), IC[] collection for Unified Command (IP-013), full FEMA demob lifecycle (IP-008), ICS-205/207/208/209 form bundle, cribbing audit lifecycle (IP-009), victim cluster linkage (IP-038), bulk-deploy mode (IP-031), 250+ SP virtualization, IST coordination, full Type I/II preset enumeration.

### What gets MORE attention in the reframe

Local departments are not a softer test case — they're a harder one per shore point. Solo IC ergonomics, field-stressed UI, and offline reliability all rise in priority:

- **Field-condition UX** — gloves, sun, wet screens, helmet-mounted use
- **Offline reliability** — local depts often have weaker connectivity than federal staging areas (no IMT, no cell-on-wheels)
- **Single-device-as-IC ergonomics** — the entire incident may live on one tablet; modal stacking and back-button behavior get first-class testing
- **Cold-start time** — first-due engine company should be able to launch the app and have a shore point on screen in under 90 seconds

## Part 3 — Decision queue for Alex

The prior draft had 7 decisions, mostly federal-scoped. The reframe rewrites them. Items marked DEFERRED move to Federal Future.

1. **Should scenario presets ship in v3.12.0 or v4.0.0?**
   - **Recommendation: v3.12.0.** They're additive (no schema migration), they unlock the local persona immediately, and they de-risk v4.0 by giving real users something to test before the schema cutover. Holding them for v4.0 packs a MAJOR release with non-MAJOR work.

2. **What's the minimum org chart for Type IV/V?**
   - **Recommendation: IC + Safety mandatory; Operations Section Chief optional and only auto-suggested when SP count exceeds ~10 or apparatus count exceeds 3.** That matches FEMA ICSSCI (SM-0322) Type IV/V guidance. No Planning Section, no Logistics, no Finance for the default case — those are scaling escalations the IC can add manually.

3. **First-due "solo IC" mode — full bypass or progressive disclosure?**
   - **Recommendation: Progressive disclosure.** Single-apparatus solo IC sees a streamlined modal with apparatus auto-selected (since there's only one) and roster controls hidden. Adding a second apparatus auto-promotes the UI to standard mode. No mode flag, no setting — derived from incident state.

4. **Per-device UID — auto-generate on first launch or prompt the user?**
   - **Recommendation: Auto-generate, then prompt for a display name on first write.** Firefighters do not want to do device setup at 0300. The UID is a `crypto.randomUUID()` stored in localStorage; the first write to Firebase prompts "Who are you?" with a single-field display name (e.g., "BC Alex"). Display name can be edited from Settings.

5. **Ship v3.11.2 release-blocker hotfix before any v4.0.0 work begins?**
   - **Recommendation: YES.** Unchanged from the prior draft. All seven findings are <1 week of work and de-risk everything downstream.

6. **NIMS terminology cutover — atomic in v4.0.0 or staggered?**
   - **Recommendation: Atomic.** Dual-write the renames in v3.12.0, cut over in v4.0.0. A staggered rename leaves the codebase carrying both names for longer, which compounds the v5.x React Native port complexity. Bite it once.

7. **Multi-agency / per-agency identity in v4.0.0?**
   - **DEFERRED to Federal Future.** No municipal department running a residential collapse needs IC[] or agency badges. Reactivation trigger: ≥10 paying departments, or a mutual-aid feedback pattern that explicitly cites this gap.

8. **Demob full lifecycle in v4.0.0?**
   - **DEFERRED to Federal Future.** Sgt. Nash's Surfside experience is the federal case. Local depts demobilize by clearing the operation at the end of shift. Reactivation trigger: a customer dept that runs operations longer than a single shift and explicitly cites missing demob workflows.

9. **Bulk-deploy mode (IP-031) in v4.0.0?**
   - **DEFERRED to Federal Future.** 220-card mass deploy is a federal TF scenario. A 20-SP local op deploys serially without friction. Reactivation trigger: any dept consistently hitting >50 SPs in a single op.

10. **Teardown Surfside sim infrastructure?**
    - **KEEP.** Do not delete `.claude/simulations/surfside-ttx-2/`. It's the federal upper-bound stress test and will be the foundation for v5.x federal reactivation.

## Honest uncertainty

Things I'm less confident about with the reframe:

- **The 2-3 week v4.0 estimate.** Per-device UID + role-based rules + schema cutover + local-scale TTX is still meaningful surface. Realistic floor: 2 weeks. Realistic ceiling: 4 weeks. The estimate compresses because federal scope (multi-agency, IC[], demob, forms) is out — not because the remaining work is small.
- **The Type IV/V default org chart content.** I have FEMA ICSSCI guidance to anchor on, but actual local-dept practice varies. The `battalion-chief` review is load-bearing here.
- **Whether the scenario presets are the right three.** "Car into building / residential / light commercial" matches the persona Alex described. There may be a fourth (e.g., "Trench rescue") that local depts hit often. Watching for this in TTX-3 feedback.
- **Schema cutover under offline-first.** Dual-write is well-understood; the edge case is queued writes from a v3.11.x client that come back online after v4.0 ships. Migration-specialist needs an explicit replay-safe path.

## What this plan deliberately does NOT do

- **Doesn't claim to replace federal-IST tooling** — that's v5.x or later. FieldShore's job in v4.0 is the everyday local case.
- **Doesn't rebuild for multi-agency from day one** — single department is the primary case. Multi-agency identity is in Federal Future with explicit reactivation triggers.
- **Doesn't ship a full FEMA Type I preset** — Type IV/V is the default. Type III is available for escalation. Type I/II preset enumeration is deferred.
- **Doesn't scope v5.0 / React Native migration** — that's the v4.0-to-v5.0 roadmap's job. v4.0 must ship cleanly in the PWA first.
- **Doesn't delete the Surfside TTX-2 infrastructure** — it's the upper-bound stress test and the seed for v5.x federal reactivation.
- **Doesn't enumerate every test case** — `qa-driver` will produce that from each release's flow surface.
